# Tarang Goyal Portfolio Brief

## Primary outcome

Help a recruiter or hiring manager decide that Tarang belongs in an **AI Product Engineer / Forward-Deployed Engineer** interview.

Product management, full-stack engineering, analytics, and data science support this identity. They are not presented as four competing career directions.

## 20-second takeaway

> Tarang enters an ambiguous workflow, identifies the pivotal product decision, builds the working AI system, and validates it with contextual evidence.

## Primary audience

Recruiters and hiring managers for:

- AI Product Engineer
- Forward-Deployed Engineer
- Forward-Deployed Product Engineer
- Product-minded AI Engineer

## Desired action

1. Read one flagship case study.
2. Download the résumé.
3. Email Tarang about a role.

## Differentiators

1. **Product judgment** — workflow mapping, product framing, scope, tradeoffs, success criteria.
2. **Hands-on implementation** — full-stack AI systems, developer tools, APIs, infrastructure, and deployment.
3. **Evidence discipline** — awards, public products, reproducible benchmarks, published research, and honest limitations.

## Content standard

Every flagship follows:

1. Observed problem
2. Product decision
3. Shipped system
4. Contextual proof

Every quantified claim must state or link to its baseline, population, method, timeframe, and ownership. Desired outcomes are never presented as measured results.

## Visual system

- Evidence Editorial layout
- Warm paper, near-black ink, restrained cobalt
- One modern grotesk type family
- Real product interfaces as the primary imagery
- Thin rules, generous whitespace, and numbered evidence annotations
- No 3D hero, gradients, glass, AI sparkles, decorative dashboards, or metric theatre
