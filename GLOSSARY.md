# Portfolio Glossary

**AI Product Engineer**  
A builder who combines product judgment, software implementation, AI-system design, deployment, and evaluation.

**Forward-Deployed Engineer / FDE**  
An engineer who works close to users and operational workflows, translates ambiguous needs into software, and stays through deployment and validation.

**Observed problem**  
A workflow problem supported by a real user, existing process, system behavior, or project constraint. Use “product hypothesis” when no direct observation exists.

**Product decision**  
The chosen intervention, why it was chosen, what alternatives were rejected, and the tradeoff accepted.

**Shipped**  
A working artifact delivered to its intended environment or public surface. It does not imply adoption or business impact.

**Live**  
Reachable and usable at a public URL. It does not imply production readiness.

**Prototype**  
A working product used to test a workflow or system. Its operational limits must remain visible.

**Production**  
A system with real users or operations plus appropriate reliability, security, support, and ownership. Do not use for a public demo alone.

**Proof / evidence**  
A public artifact, award result, reproducible benchmark, published paper, résumé-supported outcome, or documented evaluation.

**Accuracy**  
A metric that must name its test population, matching threshold, method, and sample size.

**Context reduction**  
An estimated decrease in code or tokens presented to an agent relative to a named baseline. It must retain correctness measures such as fix-file recall.

**Award-winning**  
An identifiable event and result linked to a public artifact when available.
