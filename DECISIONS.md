# Portfolio Decisions

## Decision 001: Primary positioning

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

Tarang is positioned primarily as an **AI Product Engineer / Forward-Deployed Engineer**.

Product management and analytics are supporting differentiators. The portfolio intentionally does not market four equal identities.

## Decision 002: Evidence threshold

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

Publish quantified claims only when a source, baseline, population, method, timeframe, and ownership can be stated or linked. Replace unsupported metrics with an artifact or an honest qualitative statement.

## Decision 003: Portfolio depth

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

Use three flagship case studies:

1. Studentlytics
2. Morphic
3. Locus

All other work appears in a compact archive after professional experience.

## Decision 004: Case-study format

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

Each flagship has a dedicated route with:

- role, team, timeline, and status;
- observed problem, decision, shipped system, and proof;
- constraints and alternatives;
- individual ownership;
- build progression;
- evidence methodology and limitations;
- reflection and next validation step.

## Decision 005: Visual direction

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

Use the spacious **Evidence Editorial** direction with the structured decision-trace logic from the denser concept.

Rejected:

- the compressed dashboard layout;
- the faux handwritten field-notes aesthetic;
- the 3D character as hero centerpiece;
- decorative AI imagery and unsupported metric theatre.

## Decision 006: Conversion

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-07-26

The primary conversion is an AI Product Engineer/FDE hiring conversation. The final CTA asks whether a team is hiring someone to turn an unclear AI workflow into a working product.
